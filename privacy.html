Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the intended handling of information by TSUKUYOMI OS, a local-first AI-assisted content management application under development. Features described as planned are not representations that they are currently available.

1. Information processed

Depending on features enabled, the application may process content that a user supplies (such as product images, captions and campaign details), local task and audit records, and basic TikTok account information authorized through an official login flow. If TikTok integration is enabled, access and refresh tokens may be processed to maintain authorized sessions.

2. Purposes

Information is used to prepare and review content, maintain application functionality, authenticate authorized accounts, and carry out actions explicitly permitted by the user.

3. Storage and security

The intended design is local-first. Application data is generally stored on the user's own device. Sensitive credentials should be stored securely, not exposed in public repositories or logs. Internet-connected services may process data when a user enables them; their separate privacy terms may apply.

4. Sharing

TSUKUYOMI OS does not intend to sell personal data. Data may be transmitted to an integrated service, such as TikTok, only as needed for an authorized feature. No third-party integration is guaranteed to be active during development.

5. Retention and deletion

Local content and records may remain until deleted by the user, subject to technical and backup limitations. Users should be able to disconnect integrated accounts and remove locally stored authorization data when integration is implemented. Revoking an app's access within TikTok may also be available through TikTok settings.

6. Your choices

Users control the content they submit and the permissions they grant. Requests about data access or deletion may be sent to the contact address below.

7. Children

The application is not designed for use by children under the minimum age required by applicable services.

This policy may be updated as the application develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
